# English words never given a gap's hint by one gloss (plan 081, S11),
# for what the gloss denylist can't say without losing good hints too:
# a word whose nearest gloss is another sense of a homonym ("liquor" by
# "spirits"), or only a likeness of spelling ("pump" by "pomp"). One
# line a gloss: the words, `:`, the gloss as embedded (its `text`, the
# lookup's tokenised wording), then `#` and why. A word denied its best
# gloss gets no hint at all, not its next best, which no one has judged.
# Reviewed by hand; changing it changes the gloss hash.
