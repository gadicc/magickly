# What we need from the British Library — 13 November 2026

Status: a running list, kept from 13 November 2026. Read
[current status](000-current-status.md) first.

Dee's own manuscripts would settle questions that Casaubon's 1659
print and later editions leave open. The British Library holds them,
and its Digitised Manuscripts went offline after the Rhysida
ransomware attack of October 2023. About 1,000 manuscripts returned
from October 2024. Everything we would read in them goes
here, so that one visit to the images covers it all. Items move to
"Done" with the date and the commit that used them.

## Where things stand

- **Sloane MS 3191**, Dee's own copy of the Keys (*48 Claves
  Angelicae*) and the tables, ff. 1r–80v: the catalogue record
  (https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002115575) read "Images
  currently unavailable" on 24 October and again on 13 November 2026.
- **Cotton MS Appendix XLVI**, the session records Casaubon printed,
  was never digitised. We read it only through his print.
- **Sloane MS 3188**, where the letters' names come from: what we need
  is cited already (`letters.json5`), from an archived summary and the
  Wikimedia Commons image of f.104.

Re-check the Sloane MS 3191 record from time to time. If the images
stay offline, it may be worth asking the Library whether its imaging
service can supply the pages below. Before any image is cited by URL
or used, check the images' licence terms.

## The tables (plan 061)

From [plan 061](061-gd-hall.md)'s British Library follow-up:

- the folios of the reformed table: Peterson gives ff.53v–54r, Vovina
  and Burford ff.55v–56r;
- the 1587 capitals, which `raphael1587` leaves as the 1659 print's
  small letters;
- the Black Cross's placement, and then whether it enters the data;
- the letters Dee struck through in 1584, which may explain Regardie's
  second letters that are in neither of Dee's tables;
- the two Fire squares where Sloane (p) and Cotton through the print
  (x) differ, row 8 square 1 and row 12 square 4;
- Fire's row 6 square 8 in the 1587 print: a misprint, as the version
  reads it, or the Cotton copy's v.

## The Keys (plan 073)

From [plan 073](073-enochian-keys.md); `keys.json5`'s `checked`
records list Sloane MS 3191 as not covered for every Key.

- **Dee's word for every Key row**, into each row's `sloane3191`
  reading, which the `dee` version already prefers when present.
- **The words Casaubon's print lost**: 13.23–24, 23 of Key 14's 40
  words, 14 of Key 15's 32, and 16.3–16.14. Crowley's forms stand in
  for them now, marked as his, and their division and English pairing
  are ours.
- **Dee's accents**, his continuous English for the nineteenth Call,
  and his spellings of the thirty Aethyrs.

## Doubtful readings

- **Long s read as f**: Casaubon's BUFD (Key 8) and CAFAFAM (Key 18),
  where the dictionary has BUSD "glory" and gives CAFAFAM as a variant
  of CASASAM, and CIAOFI (Key 8), where Crowley has s. `keys.json5`
  keeps the print, and BUFD's and CIAOFI's notes suspect a long s.
- **The 17 Key rows marked `uncertain`** in `keys.json5`.
- **The words Casaubon's print has damaged**: 124 rows carry the
  capitals as printed (`printed`), and 12 Keys' `checked` records name
  them.
- **Leitch's spellings that differ from our Keys' and dictionary's**
  beyond the letters the search takes as one, eight (PRGEL for
  PURGEL, TOLHAM, BAZEMLO), which
  [plan 081](081-enochian-translator.md) accepts as input (decision
  13): which are Dee's own?
- **Leitch's readings of the words Casaubon's print lost**: his
  lexicon gives Key references in Keys 13 to 16 (TOLHAM at 14.11,
  where we have Crowley's *tolahame*), presumably from Dee's
  manuscript. Until the Library's images return, should they stand
  beside Crowley's as a second reading, cited to Leitch? (For Gadi.)

The same doubts are on the expert's list
([plan 082](082-enochian-expert-questions.md)), since someone who has
read the manuscripts may answer them sooner.
