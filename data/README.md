## Sources

Most of this is correspondences, which belong to nobody. One set is derived
from an identified work and is worth naming:

**`seventyTwoAngels.json5` and `seventyTwoAngelsText/`** come from Lazare
Lenain, *La Science Cabalistique* (Angers, 1823), through the Google Books
scan in [`public/docs/`](../public/docs). Lenain died in 1832, so his text is
public domain, and **no rights are asserted over it**. The OCR of that scan is
a mechanical transcription and carries none either.

What is offered under CC BY 4.0 is the work done on top: the repaired French,
the English translated from that repair, the choice and arrangement of fields,
and the corrections. No rights are claimed where none subsist. Nothing in it
draws on any modern published translation of the work — the point of the
exercise was a copyright-free rendering of the same public-domain material.

The Hebrew names are deliberately absent: the scan cannot supply them. See
[plan 031](../plans/031-seventy-two-angels.md).
